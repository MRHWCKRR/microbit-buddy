# Micro:Bit Digital Buddy
*A pet that also protects your belongings while you're away!*

## Features
* Cute idle faces
* You can pet it
* You can feed it
* It can say hi in different languages!
* It has a built in dual layer item security feature
* Its cute!!!!!!

### Idle Faces
There are currently one face, though more can be added using a function and adding a randomSelector.
The current face is inspired by the Minecraft Shulker.

### Petting
You can interact with you pet by petting it. To pet it, apply force on the y axis, just like if you were petting the head of a dog (or your best friend Neil)
Petting works through sensing a force of 1200mg or greater on the Y-Axis.

### Feeding
You can feed your digital pet by pressing button B.
Pressing button B shows you a drumstick, along with a little animation of your pet eating it and being happy!

### Greetings
Your pet can greet you in different languages! Pressing button A shows a super happy face and a random greeting from a random language! More may be added to the randonGreeting list.

### Item Security
Your digital pet can also secure your items for you.
Just attach your pet to something while you are away and press the Micro:Bit logo. It will show a chestplate for a brief moment and will then return back to its idle state. If the compass direction is different to the compass direction when you enabled security mode, it will trigger the second phase of detection. If 1500mg or more force is applied in any direction, it will trigger an alarm as well as triggering an alert through serial monitor.

## Use as Extension

This repository can be added as an **extension** in MakeCode.

* open [https://makecode.microbit.org/](https://makecode.microbit.org/)
* click on **New Project**
* click on **Extensions** under the gearwheel menu
* search for **https://github.com/mrhwckrr/microbit-buddy** and import

## Edit this project

To edit this repository in MakeCode.

* open [https://makecode.microbit.org/](https://makecode.microbit.org/)
* click on **Import** then click on **Import URL**
* paste **https://github.com/mrhwckrr/microbit-buddy** and click import
